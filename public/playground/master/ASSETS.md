# Night Market Master food art

Generated on 2026-10-27 with the built-in image_gen tool for this project. The existing rice.webp, corn.webp and tofu.webp were inspected and used as style references. Only the new Master variant references these new files; the legacy assets are unchanged.

- noodle-v1.webp: Taiwanese vermicelli in a cream ceramic bowl with blue rim stripes.
- chicken-v1.webp: Taiwanese fried chicken cutlet in a plain paper sleeve.
- tea-v1.webp: winter melon tea in a clear takeaway cup with straw; no tapioca pearls.

All three are genuine generated RGBA artwork, not emoji/SVG substitutes. Raw generation dimensions: 1254 x 1254 PNG, alpha range 0–255. Delivery derivatives: 512 x 512 WebP, LANCZOS downsampling, quality=88, method=6, alpha preserved. No painted text; visible food names are accessible HTML labels. Full provenance and original Library identities are retained in docs/qa/night-market-master-source-ledger.json (not a public-site asset).
